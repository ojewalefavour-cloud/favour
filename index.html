    Questions

    What is the difference between the two audits?

    A manuscript audit looks at the text: structure, clarity, consistency, flow, and readiness. A visibility audit looks at the public-facing book: discoverability, positioning, listing presentation, and promotion.

    Does an audit include making the changes?

    No. You receive findings and recommendations. Carrying them out is discussed and quoted separately.

    What do I receive for book formatting?

    Professionally formatted paperback and eBook interiors with polished typography and organized chapter layouts.

    Are the sample audits from real clients?

    Yes. They are from real authors. I prepared each audit myself, and the samples here are summaries, shared that way to protect the authors’ privacy.

    How much does it cost and how long does it take?

    Both depend on the length of your book and the service you choose. Send your details and I will reply with a clear quote and timeline before anything starts.

    What should I send to get started?

    Your manuscript or a description of where it stands, the service you want, and anything you already know about the look you like.
